Building My First FPV Drone

From zero to flying in 3 weeks • June 2026

5" Plane Shaped Drone - "Grasparv"

Hello everyone! I'm a want-to-be engineer and web design student who got obsessed with drones. So I decided to build my own FPV drone from scratch. This is my full build log!

💰 Total Cost

$210

Including everything (even the tools I bought)

How I Built It

1

Week 1: Parts Shopping

Spent way too many hours watching YouTube and comparing prices from store to store like Hobby Lobby, Hobbytown USA, etc.

2

Week 2: Wiring & Assembly

I figured out a way to put the electronics together without the annoying and painful process of soldering.

3

Week 3: First Flight!

It actually flew on the first try! I was screaming with joy.

"The first time it took off, I knew all the stress was worth it."

What I Learned

  • Hot Glue Guns are annoying but get easier
  • Betaflight configuration is actually painful
  • Always buy extra build materials
  • Crashing is part of the process.
